In Firefox and Safari, opening a window ends fullscreen: open the speaker script first, move it onto your own screen, then activate Present with the option above left off.

To put the slides on the audience monitor, move this browser window onto the audience monitor, then activate Present.

While presenting, press Esc or F11 to exit fullscreen. The Exit presentation button does the same.

Fullscreen is not available in this browser or window, so Present is turned off. The slides still work in the window.
