# Epic: Course enrollment caps

**Outcome:** a course never has more enrolled students than its room holds.

**Why now:** the registrar over-enrolled three lab courses last term and had to
drop students by hand after the term started.

**Out of scope:** waitlists, and capacity rules per section.
